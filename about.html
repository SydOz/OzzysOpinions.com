The Origin of Ozzy's Opinions

Ozzy's Opinions was formed by Sydney Oswald and her brothers Daniel and Johnny. Started on October 2, 2024, Sydney believed that her and her brothers could create something for themselves and others. This website would be based upon their opinions on many different comic brands. These brands would include DC, Marvel, Image and many more. As Sydney began coding Ozzy's Opinions they stormed up ideas for what they would do to make Ozzy's Opinions great. They decided each of them would state their own opinions, and maybe the users could compare their own opinion to their's and their own. They could do what ever they wanted.
